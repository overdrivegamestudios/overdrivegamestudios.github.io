Who We Are
About Overdrive

Overdrive Game Studios is an indie studio that specializes in single player games with extremely strong and deep gameplay. We go into overdrive (without crunch) in order to make sure that every game we create is memorable, highly replayable with a lot of depth in the gameplay, and unique to anything that you played before. We really want to push the limits of what video games can do.

Overdrive all started with a passion for games. I (Noah Thomas) have loved games every since I can remember. There is something truly special about video games. I knew from a young age that I wanted to be a game designer so that I could make amazing games that gave other joy. I did graduate from Purdue Universtity with a Bachelors Degree in Game Design and Development. I'm always thinking about games and game design and one day I wanted more from the games I was playing (particularly in the horror genre). I wanted to make a game that pushed already existing concepts to new limits and games that were unlike anything that currently existed. And that's how Overdrive Game Studios and 21 Ways to Die was born.
