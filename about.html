About Overdrive

Overdrive Game Studios is an independent studio that is built around one idea. Entry level designers, programmers, artists, etc. need experience and need something more to set themselves apart in the market

We're not chasing scale for its own sake. Every project we take on gets the same intensity — from chaotic multiplaye We believe in focused, memorable experiences over sprawling ones.

Founded in, Overdrive is currently in development on two titles: 21 Ways to Die, a first-person horror game, and Gosh Jam It, a wacky multiplayer western shooter.
