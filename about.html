About Overdrive

[Dummy copy — replace with real studio story] Overdrive Game Studios is an independent studio built around one idea: small teams can make games that stick with players long after they put the controller down.

We're not chasing scale for its own sake. Every project we take on gets the same intensity — from chaotic multiplayer maps built around a single unhinged mechanic, to a solo-directed horror passion project with real-time, no-cuts kills.
