Overdrive Game Studios is an indie game studio that is commited to making high quality single player games that focus on deep and intersting gameplay. We want every game we create to be an experience that you can only get from an overdrive game. We also want to push the limits of what games can do. This could be in terms of technology, mechanics, new ideas, or just taking gameplay that works and kicking it into overdrive.
