21 Ways To Die
First-person horror action game
Overview
About the Game

21 Ways to Die opens with you and your friends infiltrating a mysterious blacklisted facility when things go horribly wrong. Now trapped and alone, you must find a way to escape the facility without getting caught by the deadly monsters that roam the facility. And once these monsters catch you, they will unleash 21 of the most brutal, horrific, insane kills you could imagine upon you.

Most horror action games give you a gun and ask you to shoot your way to victory with enemies that are designed to walk slowly and take bullets. In 21 Ways to Die, you will not have a gun. Instead the mechanic will be replaced by something else entirely designed to kick the action into overdrive.

21 Ways to Die will release in 2027. Stay tuned for more updates coming soon.
